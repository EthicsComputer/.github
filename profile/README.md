## 🔬 VAPT & Security Audit Methodology

Our assessments follow a multi-stage, human-driven offensive framework aligned with **OWASP WSTG v4.2**, **NIST SP 800-115**, and **PTES**:

```
  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │                           ETHICSCOMPUTER VAPT LIFECYCLE                           │
  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
          │
          ▼
  [ 1. Scoping & Mutual NDA ] ────────► Target specification, IP whitelisting & legal boundaries
          │
          ▼
  [ 2. Recon & Threat Modeling ] ─────► Surface mapping, technology fingerprinting & attack vectors
          │
          ▼
  [ 3. Deep Manual Exploitation ] ────► Business logic testing, chaining vulnerabilities & PoC verification
          │
          ▼
  [ 4. Executive & Technical Rpt ] ───► CVSS v3.1 scoring, business impact analysis & remediation steps
          │
          ▼
  [ 5. Remediation Verification ] ────► Complimentary re-test of patches & official Attestation Seal
```

- ❌ **Zero Automated Dumps:** Automated scanners produce 70%+ noise and miss critical logic flaws. We provide 100% human-verified proof-of-concept steps.
- ✍️ **Executive + Developer Documentation:** Clear executive summaries for boards and granular code fixes for engineering teams.
- 🔒 **Cryptographic Verifiability:** Every issued certificate has a cryptographic hash and QR code verifiable on `ethicscomputer.in/verify`.
